# EQOA Enhanced 0.5.0

- Mods: an experimental native `eqoa/1` mod API, with an Example Hearts mod. Stats Box, Target of Target, Mana Bars and HUD numbers are now bundled mods, on by default; your existing settings carry over and their keys can be rebound on Controls.
- New from the vanilla client: item links, Q-tap monster information, map places, indoor minimap floors, raid leadership marks, loot rolls and master-looter controls, Number Colors, Pet Effects, the optional server DPS meter, a Macros page, and separate touch-control sizes.
- Map and Quest Helper have master switches; group waypoints move to O, G attacks, and Space jumps on servers that offer it. GMs can Ctrl-click the map to travel.
- Fixes: severe in-world slowdown from addon state capture, overlapping ability icons in Avoids Modern UI, Tab reopening mod chat, and Wereoxxs Enter after switching windows.
- Crash reports sent to a supporting server say EQOA Enhanced; the local crash-save offer stays.
