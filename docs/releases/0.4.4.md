# EQOA Enhanced 0.4.4

Chat history with R2, richer problem reports, and the latest vanilla client.

## Fixed
- **R2 with Wereoxxs UI's chat.** Holding R2 hid the ability bar and scrolled nothing you could see. Now R2 + up/down scrolls the Wereoxxs chat, and letting go returns to the newest line.

## Changed
- **R2 keeps your HUD up.** Holding R2 to read chat history keeps the ability bar and minimap on screen, with or without mods.
- **Problem reports say what the game was doing.** F3 (or Report a bug, or the offer after a crash) still saves one ZIP to your Desktop; it now also notes your zone, target, nearby creatures and the game's mode. Your account name, password and Windows user folder are removed, and nothing is uploaded.

## From the vanilla client
- **Touch Pad Size** in Extended > Controller, for the on-screen controller.
- **Class Mastery:** Master Classes that branch (such as Spellbinder's) no longer refuse their second branch; on eqoaclassic, a Master Class's abilities can be bought again after reopening the window.
- Speed-ups and smaller fixes.

## How to get it
- **Already playing EQOA Enhanced?** It updates itself the next time you start the game.
- **New player?** Download **EQOA-Enhanced-0.4.4-Windows-x64.zip** (the ZIP, not the .exe file), extract it to a folder, and run **eqoa.exe**. Turn mods on in Options > Extended > Mods & Addons > Addon Manager.

---

Updates come from LoopRook/eqoa-enhanced releases; the game checks update.txt and verifies the download's size and SHA-256 before installing it. The standalone .exe and update.txt are for the auto-updater. SHA256SUMS covers all files. Native-Addon-Runtime-NOTICES.txt carries the Lua and Dear ImGui notices; existing MIT, OFL and third-party notices remain intact. Bundled mods come from FrontiersForge (MIT); see bundled-mods/SOURCE.txt for the small EQOA Enhanced patches (weapon-set bar, R2 chat history, Modern UI level-60 fix).
