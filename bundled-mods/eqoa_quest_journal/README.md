# EQOA Quest Journal

Off by default. Enable it in Mods & Addons (F10); it shows **Provides: Quest Journal window (Shift+J)** and
**Asks you first: drop a quest** before you do.

A Quest Journal window of its own, on its own key: **Shift+J** (the Controls page has a **Quest Journal** row, so you
can move it; a key the game, a macro or another mod already has leaves the row empty and says why). Press the key
again, press **Escape** or click the **x** to close it. It sits over play, so your character can keep walking, and the
game's own Start menu > Quests page is exactly as it was.

- It lists your quests (the game's journal holds eight). Click one to see its goals and how many you have, where the
  server sends the quest tracker (it needs **Quest Tracker** on in Options).
- **Drop quest** never drops at once. The client puts its own question, **Drop the quest "..."?**, over the screen,
  starting on **No**: press Left or Right to choose, Enter to answer, Escape for No, or click Yes or No. Only your own
  Yes drops the quest; this mod cannot answer for you, and the question ends as No after 30 seconds, when you zone or
  die, or when this window closes.
- It does not use a controller yet: no controller button opens it (Start menu > Quests still works).

When you zone, die, turn the mod off or switch on **Use the game's windows**, the client closes it for you.
