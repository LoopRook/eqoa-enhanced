# EQOA Social

Off by default. Enable it in Mods & Addons (F10); before you enable it, the list shows **Provides: Friends (Shift+F), Guild (Shift+G)**, **Replaces game windows: Friend Request, Guild Invite**, what actions it can send, and which actions ask you first.

A Social window of its own on two keys (**Shift+F** for Friends, **Shift+G** for Guild; both have rows on the Controls page so you can move them). Press the key again, press **Escape**, or click the window's **x** to close it. It sits over play so your character can keep walking, and with the mod off every game social window is unchanged.

- **Friends (Shift+F):** shows who is online first, with class, level and world. Add or remove friends, send a tell, or invite a friend to your group from a row.
- **Ignore:** view your ignore list, add players to ignore, or stop ignoring a player.
- **Guild (Shift+G):** shows your guild name, message of the day (leaders can edit it), and roster with ranks, classes, levels and online status. Invite, promote, demote or kick members, or send a tell or group invite from a row. Kicking always opens the client's own "Are you sure?" box (starting on No). Guild disband and quit keep the game's own prompt and are not offered as mod actions.
- **Who:** click **Refresh Who** to fetch who is online, filter by name, race or class, and send a tell or group invite from a row.
- **Group Search:** on servers that offer group search, search for players or groups, register or unregister your group/character, and tell or invite from a row. The **Find** button picks Auto (the game's own choice: groups when you are alone, players when you are in a group), Groups or Players. "Searching..." shows while a search is out, and an empty answer says "Nobody found". On servers that do not offer group search, the tab says so clearly.
- **Incoming Friend Requests and Guild Invitations:** shown in a compact box with a 45-second countdown. Like the game, an unanswered request is refused automatically after 45 seconds. **Accept** always opens the client's own "Are you sure?" prompt so accepting requires your own click or key press; **Refuse** declines right away; **Show in game** hands the prompt back to the game's own box.

Turning on **Use the game's windows** in Mods & Addons closes the Social window and hands any active request back to the game's own window.
